# Engineering tutoring instructions

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) first and follow its tutoring/evidence rules. Every session or resumption also requires reading:

1. [Tutoring preferences](progress/tutoring-preferences.md).
2. [Question template](progress/question-template.md).
3. [Session progress](progress/session-progress.md).
4. `progress/mastery.json`, `reviews/queue.json`, the active assessment and latest relevant daily notes as required by AGENTS.md.

Use the same case-study format and session tracker. Ask one focused question at a time. Mastery and review JSON records are authoritative; progress bars are work completion, not mastery. Do not maintain divergent Claude-specific records or infer untested skills.
